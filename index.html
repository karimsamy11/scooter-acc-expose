Keeps the gap.
Leaves the brakes to the rider.

Adaptive cruise control for standing e-scooters. A camera and radar watch the road ahead. The system holds a safe distance by limiting the motor, caps speed at the legal 20 km/h, and warns the rider before a collision. It never brakes on its own.

In the sources we reviewed, no product documents forward distance keeping on a standing kick scooter. Standing scooters offer cruise control and riding assistance, Chinese makers fit radar to two-wheelers for rear warnings, and researchers have tested forward safety filters. Our design adds forward distance keeping with the rider always in command, supervised by an independent safety chip that is planned for the hardware stage.

03

What the system does

Three behaviours, each tested in software, and one rule above them all: the rider's brake always wins.

Keeps a safe gap

The gap grows with speed: 3 m plus 2 seconds of travel. Inside that gap the motor gets no extra power.

REQ-002 · tested

Stays legal

Set speed is capped at 20 km/h, the German limit for e-scooters. At or above it, the motor gets no extra power.

REQ-012 · tested

Warns early

When time to collision drops below 3 seconds, the rider hears and sees a warning. It works even after the rider has braked.

REQ-014 · tested, threshold provisional

How it is built

SensorsForward camera, FMCW radar, GNSS, IMU, wheel encoder
Raspberry Pi 5 + AI HAT+Object detection, radar–camera fusion, logging. Not trusted with the motor.
STM32 safety chipChecks every message, latches faults, enforces limits, gives the brake priority.
Motor controllerDisabled in this version. Only through a documented manufacturer interface.

Stale sensor data, a missed heartbeat, low confidence or a rider brake removes motor authority in the same 20 ms control cycle. Status: the control and safety logic runs in simulation today; the sensors, Raspberry Pi and STM32 hardware are planned for the lab stage.

    06

    Where we fit

    Others warn about what is behind the rider, hold a set speed, or catch riders on the sidewalk. None of the products we reviewed documents keeping the gap ahead on a standing kick scooter.

    Why now

    • Accidents are rising fast, and cities and insurers want them down.
    • Edge AI has become efficient: a 2026 benchmark measured Hailo-8 as the most energy-efficient accelerator for large YOLO models on a Raspberry Pi 5.
    • Chinese makers have industrialised two-wheeler radar on e-bikes since 2024, so components exist at scale.
    • Sharing operators already pay for camera-based rider safety.

    Market size, handled with care

    Commercial reports put micromobility in 2025 anywhere between USD 42 bn and 198 bn, and e-scooters alone at USD 47.4 bn. These numbers disagree by a factor of 4.7, so we use them as context only. The business case below is built bottom-up from units and prices you can change.

    Research that came before us

    Collision Avoidance Safety Filter for an Autonomous E-Scooter using Ultrasonic Sensors
    R. Strässer, M. Seidel, F. Brändle, D. Meister, R. Soloperto, D. Hambach Ferrer and F. Allgöwer. 17th IFAC Symposium on Control in Transportation Systems (CTS 2024); IFAC-PapersOnLine 58(10), 22–28, 2024. arXiv:2403.15116, submitted 22 March 2024.

    Several low-cost ultrasonic sensors detect obstacles in front of an autonomous e-scooter. A filter handles measurement noise and missing values, and a gain-scheduled controller limits the commanded velocity to a reduced safe value that keeps a sufficient safety distance. The approach was validated in real-world experiments.

    arxiv.org/abs/2403.15116, checked 17 Oct 2026

    How our concept differs

    • Their vehicle drives autonomously and the filter limits its own commanded speed. Ours keeps a human rider in charge: the system only withholds extra motor power and warns.
    • They sense with ultrasonic sensors at short range. We plan forward FMCW radar plus camera detection, for longer range and object classification.
    • They validated on real hardware. We are at the simulation stage.

    Forward collision avoidance and speed reduction on e-scooters have already been studied. Our contribution is the rider-in-command combination, not the idea of collision avoidance itself.

    We start where safety already has a budget: shared fleets in the EU. Germany first, because its rules are clear and strict.

    A retrofit kit plus a yearly software and data service. Fewer crashes and less damage, proof of speed compliance for city permits.

    09

    Impact

    The goal is fewer injuries without taking control away from the person on the scooter.

    Safety

    Distance keeping and early warnings address speed and reaction errors in dense traffic. The real effect has to be measured in a pilot with a control fleet. We will not claim a crash reduction before that.

    Rider authority

    No automatic braking, because a standing rider can lose balance. The system only withholds extra power and warns. Brake and cancel always win.

    Young riders

    More than half of injured riders are under 25. A speed cap and early warnings help the least experienced riders most.

    Privacy

    Images are processed on the scooter. Only object lists and statistics leave it, so city and insurer data contain no faces.

    Each stage ends at a gate with evidence. We are at stage one.

    1 ConceptSimulation, tests, requirementsnow
    2 LabSafety chip, emulated motor, fault injectiongate: timing measured
    3 FieldClosed test track onlygate: safety review + site approval
    4 PilotCustom board, small fleet with a partnergate: reliability data
    5 IndustrialiseDesign for manufacture, end-of-line testgate: yield
    6 SeriesProduction and field monitoringgate: field KPIs

    In scope now

    Control logic, simulation, safety requirements and tests, sensor-data replay, bench test with an emulated motor, offline model training.

    Out of scope now

    Real motor commands, automatic braking, public-road tests and certification. These need a documented safety review first.
